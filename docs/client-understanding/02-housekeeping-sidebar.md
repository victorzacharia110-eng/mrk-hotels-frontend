# Understanding the Update · 2 — Housekeeping: A Cleaner, Simpler Menu

**For the management and staff of MRK Hotels** — **5 September 2026**

---

## 1. What was the problem?

When your housekeeping staff logged in, they saw **the same full menu meant for the front desk**. It included areas they never use — the restaurant, the bar, fun and games — while the things housekeepers actually rely on were buried and hard to find.

**What that meant for you:**

- Housekeepers had to scroll past unrelated menus every single day.
- The room-status view (which room is clean, dirty, occupied) and staff messages were not easy to reach.
- More clicks, more confusion, more training time for new staff.

## 2. What did we change?

Housekeeping staff now get their **own simple menu**, built only from what their job needs:

1. **Dashboard** — the day at a glance
2. **Housekeeping** — the cleaning task board
3. **Laundry** — laundry orders
4. **Issue Reports** — problems and maintenance
5. **Communication**
   - **Messages** — messages between staff
   - **Room Status** — the live look at every room's state

The restaurant/bar, fun-and-games and profile menus were removed from the housekeeping view entirely.

## 3. Why it matters to you

- **Faster daily work.** The two things housekeepers reach for most — Messages and Room Status — are both in one clearly-labelled section.
- **Less confusion.** New staff learn one short menu, not the whole system.
- **Fewer mistakes.** Staff are not tempted into screens that have nothing to do with their job.

## 4. What you will see in the app

- A housekeeper logging in now sees the short menu above — that's all.
- Every other role (front desk, manager, accountant, etc.) keeps its own full menu, unchanged.

## 5. Summary

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Who sees the new menu? | Only housekeeping staff |
| What is on it? | Dashboard, Housekeeping, Laundry, Issue Reports, Communication (Messages + Room Status) |
| What was removed? | Restaurant & Bar, Fun Games, and Profile links for housekeepers |
| Does anything break? | No — every other role is unchanged |
