# Understanding the Update · 1 — Restaurant: "Food Ready" Notifications for Waiters

**For the management and staff of MRK Hotels** — **5 September 2026**

---

## 1. What was the problem?

When the kitchen finished cooking an item, the **waiter who took the order had no automatic way of knowing**. Waiters had to keep checking the open-orders screen again and again to guess whether their food was ready.

**What that meant for you:**

- Meals sometimes sat in the kitchen because no one realised they were done.
- Food arrived at the table cooler than it should — poor for the guest and for your kitchen's reputation.
- Staff time was wasted re-checking screens.

## 2. What did we change?

- The moment the kitchen marks an item **READY**, the system **immediately flags that item to the waiter** who took the order — with the order number, where the guest is sitting, and exactly which item is ready.
- On the waiter's screen, the item now shows a **clear green READY (and later SERVED)** tag.
- The waiter panel refreshes by itself every few seconds, so there is nothing to reload — the "READY" tag simply appears.

## 3. Why it matters to you

- **Hotter food.** The right person is told the instant the food is done.
- **Happier guests.** Correct, prompt service reflects directly on your hotel.
- **Less rushing and confusion.** Your waiting staff no longer have to guess or ask the kitchen.

## 4. What you will see in the app

- Open orders on the waiter panel list each item.
- Items that are done show a **green READY tag**; items already taken to the guest show **SERVED**.
- No new training is needed — it is just a clearer screen.

## 5. Summary

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Who is notified? | The specific waiter who took the order |
| How? | A notification appears with order number, location and item |
| What does the screen show? | A green READY / SERVED tag on the item |
| Does staff need training to use it? | No — it is automatic |
