# Understanding the Update · 5 — Laundry: Settling Bills Correctly

**For the management and staff of MRK Hotels** — **5 September 2026**

---

## 1. What was the problem?

Laundry charges could be added to a **guest's room account without checking that the guest was actually staying in that room**. There was also no clear, deliberate "settle the bill" step in the screen.

**What that meant for you:**

- A laundry bill could end up on a **vacant room** or a guest who had **already checked out** — producing charges the hotel could never collect.
- Staff had no simple, guided way to finish an order — it was just one of many buttons.
- Orders taken at the desk for more than one item could not always be settled to the room properly.

## 2. What did we change?

- **Settling is now an explicit step.** Unpaid orders show a **Settle** button. Clicking it opens a simple choice:
  - **Mark as paid** — the guest paid in cash/card etc.; the order is closed.
  - **Post to room** — the charge goes onto the guest's room account.
- **Room posting is now protected.** The system will only post a charge to a room where the guest is **actually having an active stay**. If the room is vacant or the guest has checked out, the system **refuses** and tells your staff why.
- **Walk-in orders are covered.** If you took the order without linking a reservation (just a room number), the system works out the correct in-house stay at settlement time and posts the charge there.

## 3. Why it matters to you

- **No phantom charges.** You will never bill a vacant or checked-out room by accident.
- **Cleaner guest accounts.** Every room charge corresponds to a real stay.
- **Clear staff workflow.** Posting to a room is now a deliberate, reviewed action — not an accidental click.

## 4. What you will see in the app

- On the **Laundry** page, each unpaid order has a **Settle** button.
- The settlement screen shows the order number, the guest and the total charge before you confirm.
- If you choose "Post to room" for a room with no active guest, a clear message explains the charge was not posted.

## 5. Summary

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Where do I settle an order? | Laundry page → the order's "Settle" button |
| What are my choices? | Mark as paid, or Post to room |
| Can a vacant room be billed? | No — the system blocks it and explains why |
| Can a walk-in order still be posted to the room? | Yes — the system finds the active stay by room number |
